Crash'n Jimmy v221-A264 cumulative patch

Category 4 — Environment graphics.

A264 makes the CHASE/OTS rideable ribbon easier to follow into the horizon without adding arrows, callouts, lane paint, or gameplay changes.

What changes:
- Replaces the old single constant-width course-edge stroke with a two-layer environment-native trace.
- A soft outer halo separates the rideable surface from same-toned scenery.
- The inner edge remains restrained and sport-specific.
- Far/horizon edge contrast is slightly stronger than near-field contrast, with the largest boost on short phone landscape.
- Near-field edge treatment is intentionally quieter so Jimmy, hazards, ramps, and landing cues remain dominant.
- The pass reuses the existing CHASE projection and render-budget sample count.

Environment treatment:
- Big Bear: pale snow halo + cool blue structural edge.
- Orange County: light earth halo + dark trail edge.
- SoCal: warm concrete halo + charcoal road edge.

Unchanged:
- CHASE camera/projection/framing
- terrain geometry and ramp geometry
- speed, braking, jumping, landing, collision and scoring
- hazard/prop placement and wildlife rarity
- SIDE rendering
- desktop/touch controls
- iPhone portrait/landscape layout
