Crash'n Jimmy v221-A255 cumulative patch

Category 3 — Jimmy & animation.

A255 makes Jimmy's grounded recovery animation match A253's load-aware rough-landing physics.

Before:
- A253 could distinguish a mild rough touchdown from a genuinely heavy recoverable compression.
- Jimmy's post-touchdown animation still used only raw impact amplitude, so two rough landings with different A253 severity could look too similar.

After:
- A253 severity is latched as visual-only touchdown state.
- Mild rough landings get a smaller, quicker-looking correction.
- Heavy but recoverable rough landings show deeper first compression plus one firmer counter-correction.
- Clean, flow and timed-BRAKE absorb recoveries preserve their prior behavior.
- SIDE and CHASE use the same shared recovery envelope.

Unchanged:
- speed/stability values and hard-landing thresholds
- collision and landing forgiveness
- BRAKE/JUMP/SEND IT behavior
- ramps and terrain geometry
- scoring/progression
- hazard and wildlife frequency
- menus/results flow
- iPhone portrait/landscape rules
