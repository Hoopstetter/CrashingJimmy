Crash'n Jimmy v221-A260 cumulative patch

Category 8 — QA/mobile optimization.

A260 gives A259's ANOTHER READ crash-debrief pager a full 44px effective vertical touch lane on coarse-pointer devices without making the visible control or result card taller.

Why:
- On phones the existing result control is intentionally compact: 34px high in small/short layouts.
- That preserves scarce landscape space, but it leaves less finger tolerance than the rest of the game's large mobile controls.
- A260 adds a transparent 44px pseudo-element owned by the button itself. Taps just above or below the visible 34px control still resolve to ANOTHER READ.
- The hit lane expands vertically only, so it does not take horizontal space from Jimmy's diagnosis or overlap the neighboring landscape column.
- Text selection is disabled on the coarse-pointer button to prevent long-press selection during rapid debrief reads.

Unchanged:
- visible result-card geometry and sticky actions
- crash dialogue order and no-repeat deck
- A259 READ x/N counter and animation
- scoring, physics, progression and crash analysis
- BRAKE / JUMP / SEND IT controls
- SIDE / CHASE behavior
- wildlife frequency
- portrait/landscape layout rules
