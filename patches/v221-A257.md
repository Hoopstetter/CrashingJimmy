# Crash'n Jimmy v221-A257

Category 5 — Props, hazards & events.

A257 makes committed crossing hazards finish the crossing they visibly started.

If Jimmy does not make the early BRAKE / SET read, a staged skier, hiker, moving pedestrian, groomer, trail cart, or driveway van now keeps its authored committed crossing velocity until the actor's full trailing edge is safely behind Jimmy. Only then does it return to its base motion.

This removes the previous fixed-timer velocity reset that could make an actor visibly change intention while Jimmy was still negotiating the pass.

Unchanged:
- early BRAKE / SET yield and clear-line reward behavior
- late BRAKE cannot retroactively cancel a committed crossing
- STREETFREEZE and SoCal companion auto-clear
- event frequency, hitboxes, spacing and scoring
- wildlife rarity
- ramps, controls, physics and course geometry
- iPhone portrait/landscape behavior

The playable staging index remains A231 until the complete cumulative A232-A257 source can be written safely as one non-regressive update.
