Crash'n Jimmy v221-A277

Category 1 — Gameplay & physics.

A277 gives intentional extra ramp lift a small, bounded forward-momentum budget.

- Neutral terrain-driven ramp launches are unchanged.
- LINE SET and BRAKE-set ramp lines are unchanged.
- SEND IT and manual POP still produce their established taller/more open trajectory.
- Only lift above the same ramp's neutral trajectory pays the new forward-speed cost.
- The added transfer is capped at 0.12 game-speed units, so the change is a refinement rather than a ramp redesign.
- Validation against the authoritative v221-A268 source showed exact neutral-launch parity in bike, snow and board probes while SEND IT / POP cases paid the expected small momentum cost.

The cumulative A269-A277 source build validated successfully, but repository safety checks blocked the large index.html write. Playable staging therefore remains v221-A268 until the validated cumulative source can be applied safely.
